Sabzali Sabzov is a young Tajik singer, songwriter and digital artist born on May 7, 2010, in Dusti (formerly Jilikul), Tajikistan. He began creating music at an early age and is known for modern pop songs that blend contemporary sounds with personal storytelling. His music is available on major platforms including Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, Deezer and more. Besides music, Sabzali is passionate about technology, the internet and digital creativity. He continues to release new tracks and build his presence as one of the emerging young artists from Tajikistan.
